🚀 KeySprint

Practice. Improve. Master Your Typing.

KeySprint is an English typing practice application designed to help users improve their typing speed, accuracy, and language skills through interactive exercises and engaging learning experiences.

🌟 Features

- Word Mode: Practice typing individual English words.
- Letter Mode: Improve familiarity with English letters and keyboard placement.
- Game Mode: Enjoy interactive typing games while improving your skills.
- 10,000+ Words: Expand your vocabulary with an extensive word collection.
- 3,000+ Sentences: Practice typing real English sentences.
- 60+ Grammar Rules: Support your English learning journey with grammar resources.
- Speed & Accuracy: Focus on improving typing performance and precision.

🎯 Our Mission

KeySprint aims to make English typing practice simple, engaging, and effective.

Whether you are a beginner or an experienced typist, KeySprint helps you build confidence, improve your keyboard skills, and develop better typing habits.
